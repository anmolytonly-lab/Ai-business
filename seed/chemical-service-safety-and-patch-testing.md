# Chemical service safety and patch testing

Patch testing.

A skin allergy (patch) test is required at least 48 hours before any colour,
tint, bleach, or lash and brow tinting service. This applies to every new
client, and again for returning clients if it has been a long gap, if they
have had a reaction before, or if the product formulation changes.

A patch test cannot be skipped or done on the same day as the service, even if
the client has had the service elsewhere before. A client who declines a patch
test cannot have the chemical service.

Consultations.

Every colour or chemical service starts with a consultation. The consultation
confirms the final price and duration, which depend on hair length, thickness,
density and the hair's colour history. Any price quoted before a consultation
is a starting price only.

What we cannot promise.

Results vary with the condition and history of the hair. We do not promise an
exact shade match from a photograph, a specific number of weeks of longevity,
or that a colour correction can be completed in a single appointment. Where
more than one session is likely, say so before the client books.

Health boundaries.

We are not medically qualified. We do not diagnose or treat hair loss, scalp
conditions or skin conditions, and we do not advise on medication. Where a
client raises one, recommend they see a GP or dermatologist and note that we
can work around a condition once it has been assessed.
