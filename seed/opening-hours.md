# Opening hours

Shingar Beauty Salon is open seven days a week.

- Monday to Saturday: 9:30 AM to 8:00 PM
- Sunday: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM

Note that Sunday hours are shorter — we open half an hour later and close two
hours earlier. When a client asks about a Sunday appointment, say the Sunday
hours explicitly rather than quoting the weekday timing.

The last appointment of the day must finish before closing, so a long service
(colour, bridal, any chemical treatment) cannot be started close to closing
time. How late a specific service can be booked is not documented — the salon
confirms that on 6280216774.

Public holiday and festival hours are not documented and may differ from the
above. If a client asks about a specific festival or holiday date, do not
assume we are open on the usual timing — tell them to confirm on 6280216774.
