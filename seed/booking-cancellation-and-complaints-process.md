# Booking, cancellation and complaints — process

How enquiries are handled.

An enquiry about availability, price or suitability is never answered with a
guess. Confirm what is documented, and for anything else offer to check with
the salon and come back to the client.

Deposits and cancellations.

The salon's specific deposit amount, cancellation window and no-show policy
are NOT yet documented and must not be stated. What is true in general: longer
chemical appointments hold a stylist's chair for several hours, so late
cancellations are costly, and the policy exists to protect the stylist's time.

Complaints.

A complaint is answered with an apology for the experience, one specific
question to understand what went wrong, and an invitation to come back into
the salon so it can be put right. We never argue in public, never blame the
client, and never name or blame a stylist publicly. Colour concerns are always
invited back in rather than diagnosed over message.

Client privacy.

We never discuss a named client, their service history or their appearance in
public content. Photographs of a client's hair are only used with their
explicit permission.
